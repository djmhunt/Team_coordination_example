### 3. Good Practices Sharing (20 minutes)

Dr. Chen shared experience with Think-Pair-Share technique in Educational Psychology course
- Significant increase in student participation (90% vs 30%)
- Better quality discussions
- Students reported feeling more comfortable participating
- Full documentation: docs/good-practices/think-pair-share.md

**Committee response**:
- High interest in trying similar approaches
- Prof. Williams will adapt for large lecture format
- Dr. Patel suggested adding to teaching tips wiki page

**Action**: Create wiki page collecting active learning strategies (ASSIGNED: Dr. Chen)
